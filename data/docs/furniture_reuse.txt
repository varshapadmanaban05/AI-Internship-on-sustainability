FURNITURE REUSE, DONATION AND RECYCLING GUIDE

Furniture is one of the largest contributors to bulky waste in landfills. Most unwanted furniture has reuse potential.

REUSE OPTIONS:
- Sell online: Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, Gumtree, eBay
- Donate to charity shops: British Heart Foundation, Salvation Army, Goodwill, Habitat for Humanity ReStores
- Community platforms: Freecycle, Nextdoor, Buy Nothing groups
- Student housing networks often need furniture at the start of term

CONDITION THRESHOLDS FOR DONATION:
Most charity shops accept furniture that is:
- Structurally sound (no broken legs, frames, or springs)
- Free from significant stains, mould, or pet damage
- Compliant with fire safety regulations (for upholstered items in the UK)

REPAIR OPTIONS:
- Wobbly joints: wood glue and clamps
- Scratched surfaces: wood filler, touch-up markers
- Broken chair legs: dowel and wood glue repair
- Torn upholstery: DIY reupholstering is achievable for beginners

REPURPOSING IDEAS:
- Old drawers become wall shelves or under-bed storage
- Ladders become bookshelves or towel racks
- Old doors become desks or headboards
- Wooden pallets become garden furniture

RECYCLING:
- Solid wood: accepted at most recycling centres, sometimes chipped for biomass
- Flat-pack / chipboard: check local council — some accept, some do not
- Metal frames: scrap metal dealers or council recycling

Source: WRAP (Waste and Resources Action Programme) Furniture Reuse Guide
