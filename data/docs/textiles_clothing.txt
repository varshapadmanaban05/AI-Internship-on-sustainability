TEXTILE AND CLOTHING REUSE GUIDE

The fashion industry is one of the world's largest polluters. Extending clothing life by just 9 months reduces carbon, water, and waste footprints by 20-30%.

REUSE FIRST:
- Swap with friends or at clothing swap events
- Sell on: Vinted, Depop, ThredUp, eBay, Poshmark
- Donate to: charity shops, clothes banks, shelters, refugee support organisations
- Pass to family or community members

CONDITION FOR DONATION:
Charity shops accept clothing that is:
- Clean and washed
- Free from major holes, tears, or stains
- Not heavily worn or pilled (some organisations use these as rags)

REPAIR:
- Sewing kits handle most minor repairs (buttons, hems, small tears)
- Cobblers can repair shoes and boots
- Tailors can alter, mend, and restore garments

REPURPOSE:
- Old t-shirts become cleaning rags, tote bags, or stuffing
- Denim becomes patches, bags, or garden kneeling pads
- Wool jumpers can be felted and cut for craft projects

RECYCLING:
- Textile banks accept clothing not suitable for reuse
- H&M, Zara, and Uniqlo run in-store take-back programmes
- Specialist recyclers (Worn Again, Renewlondon) handle fibre recycling

Source: Ellen MacArthur Foundation, WRAP Textiles Reuse Report
