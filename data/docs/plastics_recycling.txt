PLASTICS RECYCLING AND REUSE GUIDE

Plastic is identified by resin codes (1-7) found on the bottom of containers.

RECYCLABILITY BY TYPE:
- Type 1 (PET): Water bottles, food containers — widely recycled
- Type 2 (HDPE): Milk jugs, cleaning product bottles — widely recycled
- Type 3 (PVC): Pipes, some packaging — rarely recycled, avoid
- Type 4 (LDPE): Plastic bags, squeezable bottles — limited recycling, many supermarkets collect bags
- Type 5 (PP): Yoghurt pots, bottle caps — increasingly accepted
- Type 6 (PS): Styrofoam, disposable cups — rarely recycled
- Type 7 (Other): Mixed plastics — check locally

REUSE BEFORE RECYCLING:
- Glass and plastic containers can be reused for storage, food prep, or crafts
- Plastic bags can be reused multiple times before recycling
- Rigid plastic containers are suitable for organising, planting seedlings, etc.

WHAT CANNOT BE RECYCLED IN MOST KERBSIDE COLLECTIONS:
- Black plastic (sorting machines cannot detect it)
- Food-soiled containers (rinse before recycling)
- Plastic film and wrapping
- Polystyrene

RESPONSIBLE DISPOSAL:
- Never litter plastics — they persist for 400-1000 years in the environment
- Soft plastics: many supermarkets (Tesco, Sainsbury) now collect these
- Hard-to-recycle plastics: TerraCycle programmes accept many types

Source: UNEP Plastic Waste Guidelines, RecycleNow UK
