RESPONSIBLE DISPOSAL OF HAZARDOUS AND SPECIAL WASTE

Some items cannot go in general waste bins and require special handling.

HOUSEHOLD HAZARDOUS WASTE (HHW) — NEVER IN GENERAL WASTE:
- Paint (oil-based and latex when wet)
- Solvents, thinners, turpentine
- Pesticides and herbicides
- Pool chemicals
- Drain cleaners and strong acids/bases
- Mercury-containing items (thermometers, some switches)
- Motor oil and automotive fluids
- Propane tanks

HOW TO DISPOSE:
1. HHW Collection Events: Most councils run free drop-off days 1-4 times per year
2. Permanent HHW Facilities: Some areas have year-round drop-off sites
3. Retailer take-back: Paint retailers often accept old paint
4. Paint reuse: Community RePaint schemes redistribute usable paint

MEDICINES AND PHARMACEUTICALS:
- Return to pharmacy — never flush or bin
- Most pharmacies accept all unused medicines free of charge

SHARPS (needles, lancets):
- Use a sharps container, then take to pharmacy or GP for disposal

ASBESTOS:
- Do not disturb. Contact a licensed asbestos removal contractor.
- Council waste sites often have dedicated asbestos skips (double-bagged)

Source: EPA Household Hazardous Waste Programme, Basel Convention Summary
