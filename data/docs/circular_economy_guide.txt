CIRCULAR ECONOMY PRINCIPLES AND REUSE HIERARCHY

The circular economy is a system designed to eliminate waste and keep resources in use as long as possible.

REUSE HIERARCHY (in order of preference):
1. Reuse As-Is: If an item is still functional, the best option is to continue using it or pass it on to someone who can.
2. Repair: Fix broken items before discarding them. Small repairs extend product life significantly.
3. Repurpose / Upcycle: Give items a new function. An old ladder becomes a bookshelf. Jars become plant pots.
4. Donate / Redistribute: Functional items that are no longer needed should go to charity shops, community groups, or online platforms like Freecycle or Facebook Marketplace.
5. Refurbish: Professional restoration can return items to like-new condition for resale.
6. Recycle: When reuse is not possible, materials should be recovered through recycling programmes.
7. Responsible Disposal: Last resort. Items containing hazardous materials must go to certified disposal facilities.

KEY PRINCIPLE: Every step down the hierarchy represents more lost value. Always try the highest applicable option first.

Source: Ellen MacArthur Foundation Circular Economy Framework
